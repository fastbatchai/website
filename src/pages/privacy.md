_Last updated_: September 28, 2026

fastbatch respects your privacy. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit this website.

## What we collect

Nothing, directly. This website has no sign-up, no contact form, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking cookies.

The only thing stored in your browser is your light or dark theme preference, so the site remembers it on your next visit. It never leaves your device.

## Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load a page, for security and to keep the service running. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

## When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don't share it or add you to any mailing list.

## Links to other sites

This site links to LinkedIn, GitHub, and Substack. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
